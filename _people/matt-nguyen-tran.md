Matt is a Bioinformatician who completed his genetics training in the Department of Genetic Medicine at Johns Hopkins School of Medicine. Originally a translational bench scientist, he discovered the vast potential of computational work during his PhD, and his dissertation work included establishing single-cell transcriptomic profiles of the human reward circuitry and integrating them with population-level genetic risk. Now a bioinformatician in the Wang Lab, Matt manages data aggregation, processing, and analysis. Outside the lab, his passions include music and dance, cooking, building community, and traveling.
