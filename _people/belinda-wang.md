Belinda Wang is a child psychiatrist and Assistant Professor in the Department of Psychiatry at the University of California, San Francisco. She received her MD from the Harvard-MIT Health Sciences and Technology program and her PhD in Biological and Biomedical Sciences from Harvard University. She completed residency in psychiatry, fellowship in child and adolescent psychiatry, and postdoctoral training with Jeremy Willsey and Matthew State at UCSF. Clinically, she sees patients in the UCSF [child](https://psychiatry.ucsf.edu/OCD) and [adult](https://ocdprogram.ucsf.edu/) OCD and Tics Clinics. Outside of lab, she enjoys spending time with her family, hiking, baking, and going to Costco.
