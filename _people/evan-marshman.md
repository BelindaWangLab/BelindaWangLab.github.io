Evan is a PhD student in the Pharmaceutical Sciences and Pharmacogenomics (PSPG) program at UCSF. He graduated from BYU with a B.S. in Bioinformatics. Prior to UCSF, he worked as a data scientist and software engineer at ZONTAL. His current work focuses on understanding the molecular basis of OCD and tic disorders by utilizing common and rare genetic variants. Outside the lab, he enjoys weightlifting, exploring national parks, playing soccer, and hanging out with his family.
